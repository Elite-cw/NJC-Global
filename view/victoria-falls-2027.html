Event Details

The grand culmination of the Women Building Africa initiative is set for the summer of 2027. CBW Zimbabwe will play host to the annual CBW event against the breathtaking natural backdrop of Victoria Falls. This high-profile summit continues its vital mission to foster digital inclusion, advance corporate communication, and champion strategic female leadership across the continent, gathering industry leaders, innovators, and changemakers for three transformative days.

Our Role

Serving on the board and acting as the core strategic partner, Joyce and the NJC Global team are managing the complete event marketing strategy, promotional content execution, and digital communications pipeline.
