Event Details

For the first time in its history, the Nzuko Africa Festival is stepping out of Paris and heading to Morocco for its highly anticipated 2027 edition. This monumental shift promises to bring African heritage, art, and corporate connections to a vibrant new setting on the continent. Attendees can look forward to a rich cultural immersion combined with high-level networking opportunities as the festival establishes its new home. Keep in touch for more updates and exciting announcements as the event approaches.

Our Role

To ensure this landmark transition to Morocco achieves maximum global reach, NJC Global is actively strategizing the comprehensive campaign and media roll-out. Our team is crafting the overarching media strategy and bespoke digital assets designed to engage an expanded international audience and elevate the festival's digital and physical presence leading up to May 2027.
