Event Details

Ikenga Day 2026 served as a vibrant celebration of culture, heritage, and the profound global diaspora connection. The European Igbo Festival brought together cultural custodians and thought leaders to uphold tradition and community values on a global digital stage. The event featured distinguished figures, including Grand Patron Chief Ben Etiaba and distinguished speaker Hon. Collins Nweke.
