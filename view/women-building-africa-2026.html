Event Details

Organized by the Commonwealth Businesswomen Africa (CBW-Africa) Zimbabwe Chapter, the Women Building Africa Summit is a landmark initiative designed to foster digital inclusion, corporate communication, and strategic female leadership. Launching its first phase in Harare, it actively paves the way for the grand regional conference in Victoria Falls.

NJC Global

Our Role

As a board member of the CBW-Africa Zimbabwe Chapter, Joyce and the NJC Global team fully directed the event organization, digital communications, and promotional strategy. The team engineered the summit's marketing campaigns, handled UI and graphic designs, produced promotional videos, and maintained an end-to-end social media presence to bridge the momentum smoothly between Harare and Victoria Falls.
