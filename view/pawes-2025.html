Our Role

NJC Global led the visual identity and awards execution for the summit. By managing the end-to-end design requirements and meticulously curating the nominees, interviewing nominees, featuring each of them and awards presentation materials, the team ensured the prestige of the honorees was reflected in a cohesive, high-trust digital aesthetic.
