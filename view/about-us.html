Partner profile · ? 01

Nia Nexus

Culture, stories, and African commerce

Jimi ObawoleFounder, Nia Nexus

Nia Nexus Africa (nianexus.africa) is a digital resource hub and cultural platform founded by Houston-based entrepreneur Jimi Obawole. Positioned as "The Diaspora's Digital Crossroads," Nia Nexus was built to connect, educate, and inspire audiences around the world on all things Africa. Rather than treating African heritage as a distant relic, the platform bridges the continent and the global diaspora, spotlighting the history, commerce, literature, fashion, and everyday innovations that link African roots to modern diaspora life.

Partner profile · ? 02

CBW Africa Zimbabwe

Women in business and leadership

Commonwealth Businesswomen Africa (CBW-Africa) Zimbabwe Chapter is a non-profit organization and national chapter of the wider continental CBW-Africa network, led by Country Head Angeline Mhlanga. The chapter officially launched its local operations in July 2025 after a year of running virtually. Its core mission is to equip women entrepreneurs and professionals with mentorship, training, digital inclusion, corporate communication skills, and cross-border business development opportunities, helping them build sustainable legacies while tapping into Pan-African and global networks.

Partner profile · ? 03

The AfrikaFora

Culture, trade, and diaspora engagement

Winifred Uloaku GaillardFounder, The AfrikaFora

The AfrikaFora (formerly known as AfricaFora) is a France-based pan-African organization founded and led by Winifred Uloaku Gaillard, an experienced events curator, intercultural life coach, 2023 Voice Achievers Cultural Award winner, and inaugural member of the ONE Campaign Ubuntu Circle in France. Dedicated to reshaping global narratives around Africa and its diaspora, the organization specializes in promoting African cultural, economic, and creative values worldwide. Its core mission is to showcase African culture and enterprise, boost intra-African trade and collaboration, and champion the continent's human and natural resources toward an independent, self-reliant, and peaceful Africa.

Past client profile · 07

Africa for Africa Women

Leadership and economic empowerment

Africa for Africa Women Empowerment (AFAW) is a South Africa-based pan-African movement founded and led by Bea Hackula, a transformational leader, mentor, founder of Assert Media (Assertive Women Magazine), and author of Arise: Be The Leader You Were Meant To Be. Built around the ethos of "Lifting as You Rise," the organization is dedicated to empowering women and youth across Africa through leadership development, mentorship, education, and community-building initiatives. Its work actively advances the African Union's Agenda 2063 and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (particularly SDG 5 on gender equality) by expanding women's participation in business, governance, and economic decision-making.
