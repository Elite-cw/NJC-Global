Privacy Policy

Last updated September 3, 2026. This policy explains how NJC Global handles information submitted through this website.

Information you provide

When you contact us or join the community, you may provide your name, organization, email address, phone number, areas of interest, and a message. Only provide information that is necessary for your inquiry.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, assess partnership opportunities, deliver requested updates, improve our communications, and maintain the security and operation of the website.

Cookies and local storage

The website uses essential browser storage to remember your cookie preference. Optional analytics should only be enabled after you select “Accept all.”

External services

Fonts, imagery, maps, newsletter tools, and social-sharing links may be provided by third-party services. Those services may process technical information according to their own policies.

Your choices

You may decline optional analytics and request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you previously submitted, subject to applicable obligations.

Contact

For privacy questions, email info@njcglobal.site.
